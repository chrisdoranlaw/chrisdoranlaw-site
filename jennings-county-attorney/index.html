Jennings County Courts

Most civil and family matters in the county go through the Jennings Circuit Court, while the Jennings Superior Court handles other case types. Both are located at the Jennings County Courthouse at 24 North Pike Street in Vernon.

If you're new to the local court system, I've put together a beginner's guide to Jennings County Court and a practical guide to parking and security at the courthouse.
