Chris Doran is a small town lawyer who listens to what you have to say and gives you options to figure out the best course of action for you to take.

Whether you have recently been arrested for a criminal offense or are currently facing a divorce or other family-related legal matter, contact Christopher L. Doran, Esq. of Chris Doran Law LLC to solve your legal needs.

Chris practices primarily in Jennings County for cases in North Vernon, Butlerville, Scipio, Hayden, and Vernon, but does travel to surrounding cities like Columbus, Seymour, Versailles and other counties for an additional fee.
