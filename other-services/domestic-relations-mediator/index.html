Domestic Relations Mediator: Finding Common Ground

Mediation is one of the most effective ways to resolve a divorce or custody dispute without the heat of a trial. As a domestic relations mediator, I don't represent either side. Instead, I act as a neutral third party. My job is to listen, facilitate conversation, and help both parties reach an agreement they can both live with.

The Benefits of Mediation in Jennings County

Lower Stress. Trials are confrontational. Mediation is collaborative. We sit down in a less formal setting and talk through the issues.

Cost-Effective. Often, mediation can resolve in a few sessions what might take months or years in a traditional court battle.

Better for the Kids. When parents can agree on a parenting plan through mediation, it sets a tone of cooperation that benefits the children long after the legal papers are signed.

Control. You decide the outcome. In court, the judge decides.

In Indiana, many counties even have programs to help with the costs of mediation for dissolution or paternity cases. If you are looking for an attorney North Vernon Indiana to act as a neutral mediator, I am here to help guide that conversation.
