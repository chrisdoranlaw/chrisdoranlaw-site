Private Judge: Privacy and Efficiency

For some, the public nature of the court system is a major drawback. Whether it's a high-profile business dispute or a private family matter, you may not want your business aired in a public courtroom. This is where the option of a private judge comes in.

In Indiana, parties can agree to hire a private judge to hear their case. This person has the same authority as a regular judge but operates outside the standard court schedule.

Why Use a Private Judge?

Privacy. Since the proceedings don't take place in a public courtroom, your privacy is much better protected.

Speed. You don't have to wait for a gap in the busy Jennings County court docket. We set a time that works for everyone.

Expertise. You can choose a judge who has specific experience in the area of law your case involves.

As an attorney in North Vernon Indiana, I offer private judging services for those who want a more streamlined, discreet way to resolve their legal disputes. It's about providing a "helpful neighbor" approach to even the most complex legal problems.
